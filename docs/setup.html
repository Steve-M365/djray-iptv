Easy Input Methods — No Typing Required

Typing long URLs with a TV remote is painful. Here are the easiest ways to get the URLs into your player.

Method 1: QR Code (Recommended)

Open your phone's camera, point it at the QR code below, and tap the link. Then share it to your TV.

All Channels M3U

Scan with phone camera

EPG (TV Guide)

Scan with phone camera

How to transfer from phone to TV:
  1. Scan QR code with your phone camera
  2. Copy the URL from your phone's browser
  3. Use one of these methods to send to your TV:
    • Amazon Fire TV Remote app (iOS/Android) — paste into the text field, sends directly to your Fire TV
    • Google TV app — use the remote keyboard feature
    • Send Files to TV app — install on both phone and TV
    • TV Quick Keyboard app — type on phone, appears on TV

Method 2: Fire TV Remote App (Fastest)

  1. Install "Amazon Fire TV" app on your phone (iOS/Android)
  2. Open the app and connect to your Fire TV
  3. Tap the keyboard icon in the app
  4. Type/paste the URL on your phone — it appears on your TV instantly

Method 3: Voice Input

Some Fire TV remotes support voice dictation. Press and hold the microphone button on your remote and say the URL. Works best with short URLs.

Method 4: Unlinked Code (One-Time Setup)

Install the Unlinked app on your Fire TV, then enter a library code to install IPTV players without typing URLs. You still need to add the playlist URLs once, but the player apps install with one click.

Prerequisites

Before installing any player, you need to set up your device for sideloading.

1
Install Downloader
Open the Amazon Appstore or Google Play Store on your device. Search for "Downloader" by AFTVnews and install it.
2
Enable Unknown Sources
Go to Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options → Install Unknown Apps. Turn ON for Downloader.
(If you don't see Developer Options, go to Settings → My Fire TV → About → click "Fire TV" 7 times to enable it.)
3
Install Unlinked (Optional)
Open Downloader, enter code 250931 to access the TROYPOINT Toolbox. Download and install Unlinked. Then enable Unknown Sources for Unlinked too.
Unlinked makes it easy to install and update IPTV players.
Note: If you can't find the Downloader app, it may not be available in your region. You can also download APKs directly from the official websites below.

Kodi + PVR IPTV Simple Client

Highly customizable, open source, great for power users

Install Kodi

1
Download Kodi
Install from Google Play Store or Amazon Appstore. Or in Downloader, enter: https://kodi.tv/download

2
Open Kodi → Settings → Add-ons → Install from repository
Select Kodi Add-on Repository → PVR clients → PVR IPTV Simple Client → Install
3
Configure the add-on
Go to My add-ons → PVR clients → PVR IPTV Simple Client → Configure
Under General:
  • M3U Play List URL: Paste the DJRay M3U URL
  • Auto Reload Playlist: Set to 10 minutes
Under EPG Settings:
  • XMLTV URL: Paste the DJRay EPG URL

Troubleshooting

Channels not loading?

  • Check your internet connection
  • Make sure the URL is correct (no extra spaces)
  • Try a different playlist — some channels may be geo-blocked
  • Restart the app

EPG not showing?

  • EPG data may take a few minutes to load after adding the URL
  • Try refreshing the EPG in the app settings
  • Not all channels have EPG data — this is normal

Buffering issues?

  • Try a playlist with fewer channels (e.g., DaddyLive or Samsung TV Plus)
  • Check your internet speed — 5+ Mbps recommended
  • Some streams may be geo-blocked in your region

How do I update channels?

  • The playlists auto-update — new channels appear automatically
  • In TiviMate: Settings → Playlists → select playlist → Update
  • In IPTV Smarters: Pull down to refresh
  • In Kodi: PVR IPTV Simple Client auto-reloads every 10 minutes
